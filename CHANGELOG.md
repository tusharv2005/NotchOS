# Changelog

Every notable change to NotchOS, newest first. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Live tab.** Live scores from the leagues you follow and a watchlist of
  stocks, indices, and crypto, with a ticker on the closed notch.

### Fixed

- The Mirror tab could close the app as the camera started.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

The first release of NotchOS: your MacBook's notch, finally useful.

- Media, Sound, Shelf, Clipboard, Calendar, Tasks, Notes, Shortcuts, Timers,
  Network, Mirror, and AI Usage tabs in the notch.
- Fixed a crash on launch for locally built (ad-hoc signed) copies.
